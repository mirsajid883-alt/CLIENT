About Us

Caring for the Lolab Valley, one patient at a time

Valley Medicate Lolab was founded to bring accessible, dependable, and compassionate healthcare to Potushai Lolab and the surrounding communities of Jammu & Kashmir. We combine experienced medical staff with modern equipment to serve every patient with dignity.

Our Mission: Quality care for all
Our Vision: A healthier valley
Compassion first
Integrity & trust

Established to serve Lolab Valley — since our founding, we've grown into a trusted name for family healthcare, emergency support, and preventive services, open every single day of the year.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What services are available?

We offer general consultation, family healthcare, emergency care, health checkups, pharmacy services, vaccination, women's and children's health, senior citizen care, and more.

Do you accept walk-in patients?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome, though booking an appointment in advance helps us reduce your wait time.

How can I book an appointment?

Use the appointment form on this website, call us directly, or message us on WhatsApp using the floating button.

What are your opening hours?

We're open every day of the week, including holidays, from 6:30 AM to 11:00 PM.
